How to win

Get the most stars. Land on or pass the star space with 10 coins to buy one. Ties go to whoever has more coins.

  • +3 Blue space: get 3 coins
  • −3 Red space: lose 3 coins
  • Aa Word space: answer a word question for 5 coins
  • ? Lucky space: a surprise!
  • ★ Star: costs 10 coins

After every round, everyone plays a word mini-game. 1st place wins 10 coins. The player who reads the most words right earns the Word Wizard bonus star.
